# Monetization Strategy

## Model: Premium + Optional Cosmetics

> **Core philosophy:** Never paywalled gameplay. Pay once or never. Cosmetics only.

---

## Primary Model Options

| Model | Pros | Cons | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Paid ($3.99)** | No friction, loyal players | Low downloads without marketing | ✅ Best for quality |
| **Free + Ads** | High downloads | Destroys immersion | ❌ Don't do |
| **Free + IAP (energy)** | Revenue maximized | Hated by core players | ❌ Don't do |
| **Free + Cosmetics** | High reach, fair | Lower ARPU | ⚠️ Consider |
| **Free (60 levels) + Paid expansion** | Best of both | More dev work | ✅ Alternative |

### Recommendation: **Free + Cosmetic IAP**

- **Chapters 1–2 (20 levels): Free forever**
- **Chapters 3–5 (40 levels): $3.99 one-time unlock**
- **Cosmetic packs: $0.99–$1.99 each** (train skins, map themes)
- **No ads. Never.**

## What Is Never Paywalled

| ❌ Never Sold | ✅ Always Free |
|---|---|
| Skipping levels | Core train types (Steam era) |
| Extra starting funds | All game mechanics |
| Time skip / energy | Tech tree (Steam + Diesel) |
| Required train types | All 20 beginner levels |
| Difficulty reduction | Offline play |

## Metrics to Track

| Metric | Target | Action if Below Target |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 retention | > 40% | Improve tutorial level 1 pacing |
| Day 7 retention | > 20% | Add daily challenge |
| Chapter 1 completion | > 60% | Reduce Chapter 1 difficulty |
| Ch.2 unlock rate | > 30% | Lower or remove Ch.2 paywall |
| Cosmetic conversion | > 5% | More visible cosmetic preview |
| Review prompt (4.5+) | After Ch.1 complete | Prompt at win screen |

## Ethical Guidelines

1. **No dark patterns** — no fake limited-time offers
2. **No FOMO mechanics** — no daily log-in energy systems
3. **Transparent pricing** — show price clearly before purchase
4. **Restore purchases** — always supported
5. **No targeting under 13** — COPPA compliant design
